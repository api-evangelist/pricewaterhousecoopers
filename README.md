PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC) is one of the Big Four professional services firms, with approximately 370,000 staff across a global network of member firms coordinated by PricewaterhouseCoopers International Limited (PwCIL). PwC organizes delivery across three primary service lines — Assurance (audit), Tax (tax compliance, planning, and reporting), and Advisory (consulting, deals, and risk). The firm operates proprietary technology platforms including PwC Halo (a suite of data analytics tools used to support audit engagements), the Trusted AI initiative (PwC's responsible-AI governance and assurance framework), and ProEdge (a workforce upskilling platform). In April 2024 PwC announced a multi-year alliance with OpenAI positioning the firm as both OpenAI's largest customer and its first reseller of ChatGPT Enterprise, supporting PwC's broader generative-AI consulting practice.

## APIs

PwC does not publish a public developer portal, OpenAPI specifications, partner API documentation, or a corporate GitHub organization. Proprietary platforms (PwC Halo audit analytics, the Trusted AI initiative, ProEdge upskilling) are surfaced to clients through engagement teams rather than as self-service developer APIs. Technical integrations are arranged through engagement contracts and PwC Technology Consulting.

The `github.com/pwc` handle is held by an unrelated individual user (Patrick Clearwater), not by the firm.
